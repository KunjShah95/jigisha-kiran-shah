# Content bank — 30 ready-to-use posts

For Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn and WhatsApp Status. Each item is tied to one page on the site, so a
viewer who wants more lands somewhere useful, and the "How I found you" line on enquiries shows which posts work.

**Before posting:**
- Jigisha reads every script in her own words. These are drafts, not a teleprompter.
- The Gujarati and Hindi scripts need a quick read by her for natural phrasing.
- Nothing here promises returns or says "best" or "guaranteed". Keep it that way. Insurance is the subject matter
  of solicitation.
- **Link format:** `https://www.jigishakiranshah.in<page>?utm_source=<instagram|youtube|linkedin|whatsapp>&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=cb<number>`
  e.g. item 1 on Instagram → `…/services/nominee-change?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=cb01`

**Video format (30–45 seconds):** hook in the first 3 seconds → one idea → one action. Film vertically, at eye
level, in daylight at the office. Add on-screen captions; most people watch muted.

## A. Short videos — English (Reels + Shorts)

**cb01 · The ₹0 form that saves months** → `/services/nominee-change`
> Hook: "This free form decides whether your family waits weeks or months for your LIC money."
> Body: It's the nominee form. With a registered nominee, a claim is a paperwork process. Without one, it becomes a
> legal-heir process. I've seen both.
> Action: "Check every policy for a nominee this week. Link in bio for how to change it."

**cb02 · My agent left — now what?** → `/guides/lic-agent-left-who-services-my-policy`
> Hook: "Your LIC agent retired or passed away? Your policy didn't."
> Body: Your contract is with LIC, not the agent. Check the status, update your mobile number at the branch, and
> pay only through LIC's own channels.
> Action: "Full checklist at the link."

**cb03 · 80C has a new number** → `/guides/lic-tax-benefit-80c-section-123`
> Hook: "Section 80C doesn't exist anymore. Sort of."
> Body: From April 2026 it's Section 123 of the new Income-tax Act. Same ₹1.5 lakh limit, but it only works under
> the old tax regime.
> Action: "Before you buy a policy for tax this March, read this."

**cb04 · How much term cover?** → `/protection-check`
> Hook: "How much life cover does your family actually need?"
> Body: A common starting point: 10 to 15 times your yearly income, plus your loans, minus the cover you already
> have.
> Action: "Free calculator at the link. Takes two minutes."

**cb05 · Lapsed policy** → `/services/lic-policy-revival`
> Hook: "Stopped paying an old LIC policy? Don't write it off yet."
> Body: Many lapsed policies can be revived within a set period, by paying arrears with interest and sometimes a
> health declaration.
> Action: "Check yours before you give up on it."

**cb06 · The one sheet every family needs** → `/services/policy-review-claims`
> Hook: "If something happened to you, would your family know which policies you have?"
> Body: Make one sheet: policy numbers, branch, due dates, nominees. Share a photo with one family member.
> Action: "I'll make it with you for free. Link in bio."

**cb07 · Utsav or Umang?** → `/guides/jeevan-utsav-vs-jeevan-umang`
> Hook: "Jeevan Utsav or Jeevan Umang: people ask me this every week."
> Body: Utsav pays a fixed 10% income with no bonus. Umang pays 8% plus bonuses that aren't guaranteed. The right
> one depends on when you need the income and how long you can pay.
> Action: "Side-by-side comparison at the link."

**cb08 · Business partner insurance** → `/services/corporate-insurance`
> Hook: "Business owners: what happens to your partnership if a partner dies?"
> Body: The family wants their share in cash; the business needs its working capital. Partnership cover pays for
> that buyout.
> Action: "Read how it works."

**cb31 · Can’t keep paying?** → `/guides/lic-revival-vs-surrender-vs-paid-up`
> Hook: "Thinking of surrendering an LIC policy? Wait. There are three better options."
> Body: Need money for a while? Take a loan against it. Can’t keep paying? Make it paid-up and keep some cover.
> Already lapsed? Revive it. Surrender is the only option that ends your cover for good.
> Action: "Side-by-side comparison at the link."

## D. LinkedIn posts (business owners, professionals)

**cb17 · Partnership protection** → `/services/corporate-insurance`
> Most partnership deeds say what happens if a partner leaves. Few say who pays the family if a partner dies.
> Partnership insurance funds that buyout, so the business keeps its working capital and the family receives its
> share in cash. It works best with a buy-sell clause in the deed. [link]

**cb18 · Employer-employee cover** → `/services/business-insurance-gidc-vatva`
> A unit in GIDC Vatva wanted a benefit that would keep skilled staff. We set up employer-employee life cover:
> the business pays the premium; each employee's family gets the protection. It suits small units too, not only
> large companies. [link]

**cb19 · New Income-tax Act** → `/guides/lic-tax-benefit-80c-section-123`
> For HR and finance teams: from 1 April 2026, 80C is Section 123 of the Income-tax Act, 2025, and it applies only
> under the old regime. Worth a line in this year's investment-declaration email to staff. [link]

**cb20 · Key person** → `/services/corporate-insurance`
> If your business depends on one person's relationships or skills, what happens to revenue while you find a
> replacement? Key-person cover is how many owners answer that question. [link]

**cb21 · MWP Act** → `/services/corporate-insurance`
> Business owners with loans: a policy under the Married Women's Property Act belongs to your wife and children,
> and business creditors cannot claim it. It's one of the most under-used protections in Indian insurance. [link]

**cb22 · 22 years** → `/about`

**cb23 · Claims** → `/guides/lic-claim-documents-checklist`

**cb24 · Agent left** → `/guides/lic-agent-left-who-services-my-policy`
