# Citations and local listings

A citation is any mention of the practice's **name, address and phone (NAP)** on another site. Google uses them to
confirm a business is real and located where it says. AI answer engines use the same signals to decide which
"LIC agent in Ahmedabad" to name — one consistent entity across ten sources beats ten blog posts.

The goal is not "as many listings as possible". It is **the same eleven facts, everywhere, forever.**

If she does only one thing from this document, it is this. For "LIC agent near me" queries, the map pack is the

- [ ] 10+ photos: exterior with signage, entrance, office interior, desk, certificates/MDRT, a consultation in
      progress (with permission). Replace the cover photo with the clearest exterior shot.
- [ ] Q&A: seed 5 real questions and answer them from the owner account — "Do you help with claims for policies
      bought elsewhere?" → "Yes…". These show in the profile and are quoted by AI engines.
- [ ] Products: one entry per LIC plan family, each linking to the matching service page.

**Ongoing (15 minutes a week)**

- [ ] One post per week, rotated: a guide link, an FAQ answer, a client story (anonymised), a seasonal reminder

**Never**

  the pattern and suspends profiles; it is also an IRDAI conduct issue.

Target: 2 new reviews a week for 13 weeks → ~95 reviews. That is the number that changes map-pack position more
than any on-page change. Keep `reviewCount` in `src/lib/seo.ts` in sync (currently 73).

## 8. Do not do these

- **Do not use lead-marketplace listings** (Policybazaar, InsuranceDekho, BankBazaar agent programmes) as
  "citations". They are competitors for the same enquiry and route it to a call centre. Different business model.
- **Do not buy a "100 directory submission" package.** Most are dead sites; the few live ones look like spam to
  Google and dilute the entity.
- **Do not use a virtual office or a PO box** to fake a second location. Suspension risk with no upside.
- **Do not vary the phone number per platform** "for tracking". It splits the entity. Track with UTMs and GBP
  performance instead.
- **Do not let a directory auto-generate a listing** from scraped data and then leave it unclaimed. Claim it or
  remove it.
