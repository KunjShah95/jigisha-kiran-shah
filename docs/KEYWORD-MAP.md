Console (queries you already appear for) and Google Keyword Planner (Gujarat, English + Gujarati). Priority below is
based on commercial intent × how close the existing page is to ranking, which is what actually decides order of work.

Status legend: **LIVE** = page exists and is indexed · **THIN** = page exists but does not answer the query properly
· **GAP** = no page yet.

## 7. Rules that keep this from cannibalising itself

1. **One intent per URL.** If two pages target "LIC policy review" and "LIC policy revival", Google picks one and
   the other stalls. Revival, review and claims are three intents → three pages.
2. **Area pages target area + service, not the service alone.** `/areas/shela` targets "LIC agent Shela"; it must
   not try to outrank `/services/term-insurance` for "term insurance Ahmedabad".
3. **Blog posts are for stories, guides are for comparisons, service pages are for buying.** A post that starts
   ranking for a commercial query should be rewritten into a service page and the post pointed at it.
4. **Titles lead with the keyword, never the brand.** Format: `{Keyword} | {Qualifier}` —
   e.g. `LIC Agent in Ahmedabad | Jigisha Kiran Shah, 2x MDRT`. The brand is appended automatically only when it is
   missing (`src/hooks/useSEO.ts`).
5. **Every service page needs one internal link from the homepage or `/services`, one from a guide, and one from
   the footer.** Orphan pages do not rank, no matter how good they are.
6. **Do not add a page for a query until the previous one has 90 days of data.** Ten half-built pages lose to
   three complete ones.
